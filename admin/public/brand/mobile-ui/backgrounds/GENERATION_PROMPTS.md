# Background Generation Prompt Set

All four production masters were generated with the built-in image-generation
tool. They are abstract raster assets with no UI, text, logos, people or
literal hospitality imagery.

## Forest Light

Create a premium abstract cinematic iPhone wallpaper in a near-black emerald
palette. Add a restrained olive-gold atmospheric bloom from the upper-left and
faint smoky emerald depth near the lower-left perimeter. Keep the center and
right side calm and dark for interface content. Use soft volumetric haze,
frosted-glass diffusion and very fine grain. Provide generous overscan for
parallax. No focal object, UI, phone frame, text, logo, keyhole, people,
symbols, architecture, watermark, generic gradient, sci-fi nebula or loud
flare.

## Glass Halo

Create a minimal boutique iOS wallpaper with dark bottle-green depth and an
incomplete translucent glass halo at the upper-right perimeter. Add faint mint
refraction and moss haze while keeping the center nearly flat and black. Use
smoked frosted crystal, controlled bloom and fine grain. No literal door, Gate,
people, UI, device frame, text, logo, symbol, architecture, watermark,
cyberpunk portal or bright neon ring.

## Champagne Satin

Create a serious financial-operations wallpaper with an almost-black emerald
base and a restrained champagne-satin reflection flowing along the lower-right
perimeter, like light moving across precision metal behind smoked glass. Keep
the title and transaction-list regions dark. No numbers, currency symbols,
charts, coins, gold bars, UI, text, logos, people, architecture, watermark,
crypto aesthetic or glossy luxury-advertising treatment.

## Marine Refraction

Create a non-literal spatial wallpaper with deep marine-teal and forest-black
depth. Add extremely soft water-like refractions at the bottom-left and
upper-right edges with a muted moss horizon haze below center. Keep the main
reading area dark. Suggest place and movement without depicting a destination.
No literal boat, building, room, villa, hotel, landscape, people, UI, text,
logo, watermark, tropical water, resort imagery or yacht advertising.
